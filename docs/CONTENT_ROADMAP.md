## Longer-term collection plan

La Fontaine content is selected editorially rather than produced as a compulsory edition of every fable. Additional popular fables may be added individually after an explicit selection, while preserving their canonical book and fable order in the library.

## Canonical-source candidates

- *La Parure*: <https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Contes_du_jour_et_de_la_nuit_(%C3%A9d._Flammarion,_1885)/La_Parure>
- *Cendrillon*, complete 1697 edition: <https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Histoires_ou_Contes_du_temps_pass%C3%A9_(1697)/Original/Cendrillon>

Source transcription, navigation labels, annotations, and editorial expansion must be separated before creating the immutable canonical text. No English sentence translations are stored or displayed.

For *Cendrillon*, the long `ſ` is rendered as `s` and the ampersand as `et`. Historical spelling and vocabulary are otherwise retained. This keeps the complete original—including its second moralité—without silently combining readings from different editions.
